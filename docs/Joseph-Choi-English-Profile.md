## Expanded artist story (English)
For Joseph Choi, magic is more than a surprising illusion. It can be a shared language that invites people to laugh, pay attention, communicate, and experience wonder together. Drawing on years of performing and teaching, he explores magic as a creative tool for education, human connection, and healing-oriented community activities.

His work and interests span performances, educational magic, audience participation, and the evolving relationship between magic and new technologies. Through the Korean Magic Society AI Webzine initiative, he hopes to help performers share real-world stage experience, improve performance conditions, exchange ideas respectfully, and discover new opportunities for sustainable artistic careers.
